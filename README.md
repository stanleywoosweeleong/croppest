# Perosak Tanaman · Crop Pests · 作物病虫害

A small offline-capable phone app (PWA) of crop pests and diseases in Sabah, in **Bahasa Melayu, English and 中文**.

The content and photos come from the **Smart Tani Perosak Dan Penyakit** library of
**Jabatan Pertanian Sabah** (https://smarttani.sabah.gov.my/ALUS/Pest/). That site is in Malay and English;
this app adds Chinese. The Malay text is the original; English and Chinese are translated from it.
This is an unofficial learning app.

What's inside:

- **Crops:** 14 crops with their pests and diseases (photos, description, management advice, symptoms by plant part, confirmation steps).
- **Diagnose:** choose the crop and the damaged part, then compare the photos and symptoms.
- **Type tags:** each problem is marked insect, fungus, water mould, bacteria, virus, nematode, alga, lichen or animal, with a filter on each crop.
- **Look-alikes:** each pest page lists other problems on the same crop and plant part, to practise telling them apart.
- **Learn:** a photo quiz (name the cause), a type quiz (what kind of organism), and flashcards. Progress is saved on the phone; pests you miss come back more often.
- **Guide:** what each type of cause is, how to recognise it and how it is controlled, plus a glossary of terms, in all three languages (`guide.js`).
- **Search:** finds any pest in any of the three languages.
- **Offline:** Settings → *Download all photos* saves everything on the phone for use in the field.

Open the published site on a phone and use **Install** (Android) or **Share → Add to Home Screen** (iPhone).

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app (layout, styles, logic).
- `i18n.js`: interface text in `ms`, `en`, `zh`. Keep the three in step.
- `data.js`: pest content in three languages, generated by `tools/build.py`. Don't edit it by hand.
- `img/`: photos (full size and `img/t/` thumbnails), converted from the Smart Tani originals.
- `sw.js`: network-first service worker; photos are cached on first view.
- `tools/`: `fetch.py` downloads the Smart Tani pages, `parse.py` turns them into `data/data.json`,
  `build.py` merges the translations in `data/tr/` and writes `data.js` and `img/`.

## Updating the content

```
python3 tools/fetch.py data          # download pages and photos (skips files already there)
python3 tools/parse.py data          # -> data/data.json
# translate new or changed pests into data/tr/*.json (see data/TRANSLATE.md)
python3 tools/build.py data .        # -> data.js and img/
```
